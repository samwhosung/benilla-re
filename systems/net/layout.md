# net: layout

Where the system's code lies, what runs it at startup, and what nothing runs.

net owns three runs of code. The first, `0x536eb0` to `0x5388a0`, holds `NetClient.cpp` and
`NetInternal.cpp`. The second, `0x5a9e00` to `0x5be840`, holds in order `ClientServices.cpp`,
`FriendList.cpp`, the account data and download code, `ChecksumExecutables.cpp`, `Login.cpp`, the
realm connection, `WowConnection.cpp`, `WDataStore.cpp`, `Grunt.cpp` and `AddressList.cpp`. The
third, `0x5d1420` to `0x5d3f20`, holds the SHA-1 code, the SHA-1 random pool, SRP6 and its
big-number helpers. The files of the first two runs open their code with their static initializers;
the third run's code has one, the +∞ `0x5d3b20` near its end, and starts at `0x5d1420`, the SHA-1
compression, right after IGxuFontGlyph.cpp's last initializer. Of the 1,146 functions in the three
runs, 552 are those initializers and their thunks, and 81 are unreached. The roots are the
static initializers and every function that another system's code calls or an aligned data
dword names. All 321 primitives in the closure have a bit-exact test.

The system's other docs are `wire`, `threads`, `dispatch`, `messages`, `session`, `connection`, `login`, `logonlink`, `crypto`, `friends`, `who`, `accountdata` and `download`.
