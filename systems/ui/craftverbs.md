# ui: craft window verbs

The Lua functions of the craft window (`CraftInfo.cpp`) are documented in `tradeskill/craft`: open, close and selection, names and skill line, rows, difficulty, cost and level, reagents and links, spell focus and description, collapse and cast. The rows and their order are `ui/craft`; `GetCraftIcon` is `ui/spellbook#recipe-icons`; the profession window's twins are `tradeskill/bindings`.

Roots: `0x4f6000`, `0x4f6c10`, `0x4f6cb0`, `0x4f70f0`, `0x4f7160`, `0x4f7210`, `0x4f72a0`, `0x4f74d0`, `0x4f78b0`, `0x4f7a90`, `0x4f7d10`
